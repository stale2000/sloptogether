## Requirements retained

- Normal story clips are approximately 15 seconds, 16:9, with browser playback captured through standard OBS window capture. Subscriber alerts are approximately **10 seconds**.
- Main/output pages are large, clean playback surfaces with seek/history and click-to-pause. Moderation is separate; the independently packaged Twitch Extension handles prompt participation and voting.
- One Twitch channel can own multiple independent streams. History and controller authority remain scoped to the chosen stream.
- The same worker program supports generation and upscale roles on Fal/Linux and Windows/WSL2. Ordinary contributors pair with Twitch and retain human review; scoped trusted owner workers can receive policy approval after screening and validation.
- Comfy Cloud prompt/image requests require an explicit streamer/moderator action. Automated stories and subscription alerts reuse approved images; they never automatically spend the shared Comfy key.
- Use the imported FastH3 Trim five-step and SoL paths; no Wan workflow. Use the existing 512×288 story base profile and 1184×666 upscale candidate. Normalize low-cost keyframes explicitly to the recipe geometry without stretching.
- A consumed payment buys one render attempt. Rejection does not refund render authority; uncertain provider submission is reconciled before another submission.

## F. Deliver the Bits prompt queue and finish public operations

The workspace now has a fixed-SKU Bits queue-entry path. It screens the viewer's prompt, creates a hidden pending-payment proposal, binds the short-lived intent to the viewer/channel/stream/SKU and proposal, and only makes the proposal eligible for the normal vote after Twitch's authoritative transaction is verified. A paid queue entry does not buy a win or a render. Free prompt submission remains available. H3 Max Turbo is a separate action and remains disabled.

This design records the policy distinction more accurately: Twitch's official Bits examples include Queue+ (Bits queue entry and priority with customizable fields), BitPoll (a viewer-created poll), and View Message Alert (viewer messages). These are precedents for user content within fixed app-defined Bits interactions, not blanket approval of arbitrary user-specified paid outcomes. The queue action, fixed catalog product, moderation path, and vote are defined by SlopTogether; the prompt is screened content. Never describe Extension Bits as a donation or promise a USD equivalent. Sources: [Twitch policy](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/extensions/guidelines-and-policies/), [official Bits-enabled Extensions](https://dev.twitch.tv/extensions/bits-extensions-to-try/).

Production stays gated by SLOPTOGETHER_BITS_PROMPT_QUEUE_ENABLED, SLOPTOGETHER_BITS_POLICY_APPROVED, SLOPTOGETHER_BITS_EVENTSUB_READY, and the fixed catalog JSON. As checked October 31, 2026, the Twitch version is still Local Test and Bits monetization is not enabled. Local/Hosted Test transactions do not spend real viewer Bits; do not claim live monetization until the Extension is released and the catalog/transaction path is configured. See [the queue economics and fulfillment plan](paid-turbo-segments.md).
